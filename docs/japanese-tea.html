Whisking your own matcha is easier than it looks: warm water, a bamboo whisk (chasen), and thirty seconds of gentle "W" motions until it froths. The taste is nothing like café matcha lattes — fresher, greener, calmer. A starter set with the whisk, the bowl (chawan), and ceremonial-grade powder is everything you need for your first proper bowl.
